Digitalisation and information management

This online tool provides the contact details and websites of AIS/AIM providers and regulators. The States/territories* are listed alphabetically. The search functionality allows users to quickly find what they are looking for - just start typing and watch the list get refined with each character.

The information found on this page is provided by the users of the '@is online' and may not be complete or accurate. If you are aware of a missing, outdated or wrong AIS website, please contact us.
