Aeronautical Information Service of Georgia ensures flow of aeronautical data and aeronautical information, flight plan and associated messages management, aeronautical charts creation/update and flight procedure design. 

AIS forms part of “Sakaeronavigatsia” Ltd and consists of Aeronautical Information Publication office, Central Briefing-NOTAM office, Batumi and Kutaisi Briefing offices.
